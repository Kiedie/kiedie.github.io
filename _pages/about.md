Hi! 😊 My name is Juanjo. I'm passionate about mathematics, physics, and artificial intelligence. Beyond academia, I am also an enthusiastic practitioner of extreme sports, such as diving and skiing.

I'm a PhD candidate in the [Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://decsai.ugr.es/en) at the [University of Granada](https://www.ugr.es/en), Spain. My PhD thesis focuses on zero-shot learning, with particular emphasis on preprocessing the semantic space. My supervisors are [Isaac Triguero Velázquez](https://scholar.google.es/citations?user=KogUP4YAAAAJ&hl=es), [Guillermo Gómez-Trenado](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8n0IFOIAAAAJ&hl=es), and [Francisco Herrera Triguero](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HULIk-QAAAAJ&hl=es) at the University of Granada. I have also collaborated with [Shreyank N. Gowda](https://sites.google.com/view/shreyankngowda/home?authuser=0) at the University of Nottingham, where I was a visiting PhD student.

I hold a [BSc in Mathematics](https://www.ugr.es/en/study/undergraduate/bachelors-degree-mathematics), a [BSc in Computer Science](https://grados.ugr.es/informatica/docencia/plan-estudios), an [MSc in Quantum Computing](https://www.nebrija.com/programas-postgrado/master/computacion-cuantica/), and an [MSc in Data Science and Computer Engineering](https://masteres.ugr.es/datcom/docencia/plan-estudios).

Check out my [research](/research/) for the full list of my work, or my [CV](/cv/) for a quick overview of my academic journey. In [Outreach](/outreach/) you can find a list of my outreach activities, including talks and appearances in news and media. In [Teaching](/teaching/) you can find a list of the courses I have taught and BSc / MSc theses I have supervised.

If you would like to get in touch, email me at [jh16357@gmail.com](mailto:jh16357@gmail.com) or [juanjoha@ugr.es](mailto:juanjoha@ugr.es). I'm always happy to chat about research, collaborations, or just to say hi! 😊
